Hospital Wing 4B

A head-coupled 3D window into a haunted hospital corridor. Your webcam tracks your face so the view follows your head; the mouse works too.

Uses your camera. It asks for webcam access to track your head. Nothing is recorded or uploaded: the video is analysed live in your browser and discarded. You can skip the camera and play with the mouse.
